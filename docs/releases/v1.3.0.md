# BondScore v1.3.0 - Direct Messages & Group Chats Release

A major release introducing private Direct Message analytics, group chat energy tracking, the Fun Relationship Progression System, and combined timeline + DM bond intelligence.

## 🌟 What's New in v1.3.0

### 1. Direct Messages & Group Chats Layer
- **Basic Analytics (100% Private, On-Device)**:
  - Sent vs Received message counts per conversation.
  - Reply rate percentage & median reply turnaround times.
  - Actionable **"Waiting for your reply"** list showing pending inbound messages with elapsed waiting duration and one-click "Reply on X ↗" buttons.
  - Inbox filter pills (`All`, `⏳ Waiting on You`, `🔥 Streaks`, `🧊 Going Cold`, `👑 Closest Friends`, `👥 Groups`).

- **Fun Relationship Progression System**:
  - **1:1 Conversation Bond Score (0–100)**: Mathematically weighted by message frequency, 50/50 reciprocity balance, recency decay curve, and reply speed.
  - **5 Progressive Relationship Ranks**:
    - 👑 **Ride or Die** (91–100)
    - 💖 **Bestie** (76–90)
    - 🔥 **Close Friend** (51–75)
    - 🤝 **Friend** (26–50)
    - 👋 **Acquaintance** (0–25)
  - **Daily Messaging Streaks**: Tracks consecutive daily conversations with visual streak badges.
  - **Fading Warnings**: Soft alerts when a high-bond conversation has gone quiet for several days.
  - **"Who Should I Message?" Smart List**: Highlights cooling relationships so you can reach out and keep bonds active.
  - **"Closest Friends" Private Leaderboard**: Ranked view of your strongest 1:1 direct message relationships.

- **Group Chat Vitality & Energy**:
  - **Group Energy Score (0–100)** with dynamic levels:
    - ⚡ **Buzzing** (80–100)
    - 🔥 **Active** (55–79)
    - ☕ **Chill** (30–54)
    - 🍂 **Cooling** (15–29)
    - ❄️ **Dying / Inactive** (0–14)
  - **Most Active Members Roster**: Breakdown showing each participant's message share percentage.
  - **Dying / Inactive Alert**: Detects when once-active group chats become dormant.

### 2. Combined Experience
- **Overall Relationship Strength**: Seamlessly unites Public Timeline Mutual scores with Private DM health.
- **Intimacy Synergy Bonus**: Relationships active both publicly on the timeline and privately in DMs receive an automated synergy bonus.
- **Privacy First Guarantee**: 100% on-device metadata storage in `chrome.storage.local`. Message content is never stored remotely or sent to any server.
- **Customizable Preferences**: Dedicated Settings section to toggle DM tracking, adjust fading warning thresholds (1–14 days), and tune the DM weight in combined scoring.

### 3. Premium UI & Badges
- In-page DM conversation row badges and open thread header pills.
- Interactive DM scorecard modal with spring transitions, progression roadmap, and reciprocity balance bars.
- Emil Kowalski / Apple-grade craft with dark, dim, and light theme resilience.
