# BondScore v1.5.9 — Release Notes

## 🚀 Highlights & Improvements in v1.5.9

### 1. Orbit View Overhaul & Profile Avatar
- **Capacity-Aware Orbit Geometry:** High mutual counts no longer overcrowd the outer orbit ring into an illegible cluster. Orbit tiers dynamically space visible nodes with breathing room and clearly indicate remaining mutuals (`+X more in your CRM`).
- **Profile Avatar in Center Sun:** The center sun of your orbit now displays your actual profile avatar rather than a generic text placeholder, surrounded by a smooth atmospheric glow.

### 2. Group Chat Energy Activity Calibration
- **Strict 20-Hour Activity Gates:**
  - **Hyperactive (95–100):** 99+ messages in the last 1 hour.
  - **Energetic (80–94):** 50+ messages in the last 20 hours.
  - **Active (60–79):** More than 20 messages in the last 20 hours (`recent20h >= 21`).
  - **Steady (40–59):** Regular day-to-day chatter.
  - **Chill (20–39):** Quiet banter.
  - **Dying / Inactive (0–19):** Automatically flagged when fewer than 21 messages occur in 20 hours.
- Direct messages and groups are clearly decoupled: group chats never trigger "Waiting on You" reply reminders.

### 3. Deep Conversation Analysis Resiliency
- Fixed concurrent deep scan race conditions in `core/deep-analyzer.js` by reusing in-flight analysis promises, preventing scan warnings or state desynchronization.

### 4. Cross-Platform Polish (Threads & Bluesky)
- Streamlined badge layout beside usernames.
- Compact micro-badges on Bluesky (`🤝 score`) to preserve space for long handles.
- DOM signature hashing prevents badge flickering across live feed updates.
